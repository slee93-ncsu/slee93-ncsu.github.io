Datacenter Under review

Spatiotemporal Characterization and Short-Term Forecasting of Inlet Temperature in a GPU-Intensive Data Center

Using one month of operational data from 980 GPU nodes, this study asks two separate questions: which nodes run persistently warm, and how a node's inlet temperature will change over the next 5–30 minutes. Physical location answers the first; recent temperature history answers the second.

Where the heat sits

Study details

  • Data: public operational dataset from a GPU-based HPC facility (Borghesi et al. 2023) · July 2022 · node inlet temperature and power at ~1-min intervals
  • System: 980 nodes · 49 racks in three rows · 20 vertically stacked nodes per rack
  • Jobs: 411,348 scheduler records linked to nodes by time and assignment
  • Spatial analysis: regression with natural cubic splines on horizontal and vertical position, validated by rack-level cross-validation
  • Forecasting: gradient-boosted trees predicting the temperature change 5, 10, and 30 min ahead · trained Jul 1–21, tested Jul 26–31
  • Authors: Seungmin Lee, Juwan Ha, Yujin Kim, Jaeyoon Byun, Soolyeon Cho
